Works / AVS-002

Inheritance Without Memory: Notes Toward a Biophysical Theory of Biological Archives

Document statusFictional / diegetic scholarly article · unsubmitted · unpublished · not a validated scientific result.
Attributed authorArthur Valerius Sterling
Date1972-06-09
PlacePrinceton
LayerHypothesis
ProvenanceAttributed paper in the fictional Sterling archive

Abstract

This paper examines whether a demanding possibility can be formulated without confusing heredity with autobiographical memory. Sterling distinguishes physical persistence, inherited differences, altered development or behaviour, and the conjectural possibility of a reportable experience.

Its central difficulty is whether any heritable carrier possesses a writer, a stable code, a mechanism of preservation, and a reader. Sequence, chromatin, regulatory chemistry, cytoplasmic inheritance, and developmental dynamics may form a richer state space, but that state space is not thereby a diary.

Inheritance is not memory; a disposition is not a recollection; a molecular trace is not a readable document.

Editorial note

This paper is speculative and unresolved. No evidence establishes that an ancestral event can be transmitted as a conscious scene, and the paper offers no mechanism for reading one.
