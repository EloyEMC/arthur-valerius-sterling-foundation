LayerThese are editorial interventions in the archive. They are not statements by Arthur or Elizabeth.

The papers are hypotheses

The 1963 and 1972 papers are attributed, unsubmitted, unpublished hypotheses. Their references do not establish their claims.

Editorial note · current

Elizabeth must not disappear into Arthur

Elizabeth Crawford's historical work and professional life are independent parts of this archive. Her relation to Arthur is personal and intellectual, not scientific collaboration.

Editorial principle · current

Elizabeth's provisional work records

Three web records make Elizabeth’s independent historical activity discoverable. Each keeps its form and provisional status visible.

Provisional record · current

Kate’s 2008 starting point

Catherine “Kate” Sterling initiated the website as a measured family and legacy record after Elizabeth’s death around 1984 and Arthur’s disappearance in 1991. The first site used family papers and available materials; later cataloguing additions are not silently projected back into the 2008 Foundation record.

Editorial note · current

The collection is larger than the catalogue
