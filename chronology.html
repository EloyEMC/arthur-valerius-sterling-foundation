Chronology

A shared chronology, deliberately incomplete

Reading noteDates and intervals carry their own certainty. Unknown years remain unknown; this list is ordered by established date. Undated work records appear separately at the end.

1918 · Cambridge

Arthur Valerius Sterling is born. biographical record · established

1921 · Britain

Elizabeth Crawford is born. Her exact birthplace is not established here. biographical record · incomplete

1948–1953 · Cambridge to Princeton

Arthur and Elizabeth meet in Cambridge, marry in 1951, and move together to Princeton in 1953. shared record · established

1962 · Rome

Elizabeth makes a professional journey to Rome; the precise occasion remains pending. biographical record · partial

18 October 1963 · Princeton

Causal Structure and the Physical Address of a Past State is dated. work · hypothesis · unsubmitted

9 June 1972 · Princeton

Inheritance Without Memory is dated during Arthur's interdisciplinary period. work · hypothesis · unsubmitted

1975–1978

Elizabeth undergoes major treatment for breast cancer; Arthur and Elizabeth separate temporarily in 1976, then resume living together and rebuild their marriage. Medical details remain pending. personal record · incomplete

c. 1984–1988 · Princeton, then Bétera

Elizabeth dies around 1984. Arthur continues in Princeton, retires in 1988, and later works on CÓDIGO TEMPUS in Bétera with books and papers. partial · unresolved details

14 March 1991

Arthur officially disappears. His fate remains unresolved. personal record · unresolved

2008 · Foundation website

Catherine “Kate” Sterling initiates a modest public website from family papers and available materials. It is intended to grow gradually as descriptions become possible; later catalogue additions are not treated as part of the original 2008 record. editorial project · established

Undated provisional work records

Letters in Motion, The Afterlife of a Text, and Routes of Persistence are undated, provisional web records for Elizabeth's historical materials. They are not placed within the dated sequence.
